# T4.04 review

Lesson: `part4-cka/T4.04-manage-the-lifecycle-of-kubernetes-clusters.md`. Checked against the topic entry, `00-conventions.md` (including section 9) and summary section 2.

## 1. Coverage

Every subtopic bullet, key command, common mistake and lab is present. These are missing or thin:

- Restoring etcd: the official procedure says to stop or restart the API servers and then kube-scheduler, kube-controller-manager and kubelet after a restore. The lesson never says this.
- No troubleshooting path for a restore that fails or leaves etcd empty. Examples are a typo in the `hostPath` (kubelet's `DirectoryOrCreate` makes an empty dir, so etcd starts empty), etcd in CrashLoop after the edit, or wrong ownership. Section 6.1 only covers upgrades.
- Done criteria "real outputs of plan/apply/snapshot status" and "runbook verified end-to-end on 1.36.5 -> 1.37.1" are NOT met. The lesson says so honestly and lists every output as `EXPECTED OUTPUT (not captured)`.
- `etcdctl endpoint health` and `member list` are only named in a table. They are never demonstrated, for example to confirm health after a restore.
