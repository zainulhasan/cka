| major | 2.9, "The problem" paragraph | The mechanism is stated wrongly (conventions section 10: never state a mechanism you cannot confirm). It says traffic from Pod A to a ClusterIP whose backend is Pod B on the same bridge "needs kube-proxy's iptables rules to see it. Without them, the packet is not rewritten". A Pod-to-ClusterIP packet is addressed to the bridge's gateway MAC, so it is routed by the host and hits PREROUTING normally. The DNAT does happen. What breaks is the hairpin: after DNAT the packet goes back out the same bridge to Pod B, and Pod B's reply is switched at L2 directly to Pod A without passing conntrack, so it is never un-DNATed. Pod A receives a reply from Pod B's IP instead of the ClusterIP and drops it. | Rewrite the paragraph to describe the reply path: bridged frames skip iptables/conntrack, so the reverse translation is missed; `br_netfilter` plus `bridge-nf-call-iptables=1` makes bridged frames traverse the hooks. State the symptom (Service works from other nodes, fails from a Pod on the same node as the backend), as 2.9 already does later. |
| minor | 2.11, overhead table (VXLAN row) | "50 (outer Ethernet 14 + outer IPv4 20 + UDP 8 + VXLAN 8)" mislabels the 14 bytes. The outer Ethernet header is not counted against the underlay MTU. The 14 bytes are the inner Ethernet header carried inside the VXLAN payload. The total (50) and the 1450 result are right. | Change to "inner Ethernet 14 + outer IPv4 20 + UDP 8 + VXLAN 8", and add a half sentence that the VXLAN payload is a whole Ethernet frame. |
