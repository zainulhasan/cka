## Findings

| Severity | Section | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| major | 2.4 (podManagementPolicy paragraph) | Quote: "`podManagementPolicy` (`OrderedReady` default, or `Parallel`; it controls scale up/down and also whether a rolling update is strictly one at a time)". This is wrong. The official StatefulSet docs say `Parallel` affects only scaling operations; updates are not affected. A reader would think `Parallel` speeds up rolling updates. | Say that `podManagementPolicy` governs scale up/down and Pod deletion order only. Rolling updates go one Pod at a time, high ordinal to low, regardless of the policy. Keep the "Forced rollback" warning tied to `OrderedReady`. |
| major | 2.3 "Under the hood", section 6 row 3, 6.3 steps 4-5 | The lesson states as fact that an untolerated taint leaves the node "without a daemon Pod, counts as unavailable, and uses up the `maxUnavailable` budget. The rollout then stalls." Section 6.3 then admits this "was not observed on v1.37". Conventions section 10 says never state a mechanism you cannot confirm. Also, the DaemonSet controller only counts nodes where the Pod should run, so a node that no longer tolerates a taint most likely drops out of `DESIRED` instead of stalling the rollout. The reliable stall causes are a Pod that exists but is not available: `Pending` (insufficient resources), image pull failure, or a failing probe. | In 2.3 and row 3, lead with the Pod-exists causes (resources, image, probe). Move the taint case to "a node that cannot run the Pod may silently drop out of `DESIRED`; check `k get pods -o wide` against `k get nodes`". Mark the budget claim as to verify. Keep the entry's "fix that node" action. |
| minor | 2.6 | `k get deploy,ds,sts -A` is said to expose "UP-TO-DATE or READY below the wanted count". `k get sts` has no `UP-TO-DATE` column, and sections 2.6/9 tell the learner to watch `READY`/`UP-TO-DATE` for StatefulSets. Cluster-wide stuck-StatefulSet detection is therefore not actually shown. | Add: `k get sts -A -o custom-columns=NS:.metadata.namespace,NAME:.metadata.name,CUR:.status.currentRevision,UPD:.status.updateRevision,UPDATED:.status.updatedReplicas,READY:.status.readyReplicas` (different CUR/UPD means a pending or held update). |
